Hello!

Welcome to Nano-Interface Electronics Lab (NIE LAB) at Pukyong National University (PKNU).

When two different materials meet, molecules move across the boundary, line up, and change what the material can do. We study these phenomena at interfaces and apply them to real devices, to find something scientifically FUNNY, INTERESTING, and RESULTS that help in our real life.

What we study: Interfacial phenomena · Molecular ordering · Mass diffusion · Physical chemistry
