What you will learn

Device fabrication

Organic electrochemical transistors, field-effect transistors, neuromorphic devices and wearable sensors — from solution to working device.

Materials design

Conjugated polymers, ion gels, dopants and interlayers: how molecules order, how ions diffuse, and how interfaces control both.
